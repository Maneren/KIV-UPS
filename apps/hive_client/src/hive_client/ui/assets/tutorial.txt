Hive - how to play

* Drag a piece from the left palette onto the board. The first piece must go at the board center (0, 0).

* Drag your own piece to a green ring to move it. Hover a piece to preview its legal moves.

* Place your Queen before moving any piece.

* Queen moves 1 step.
* Spider moves exactly 3 steps around the hive.
* Beetle moves onto any neighbouring occupied cell.
* Grasshopper jumps over a line of pieces.
* Ant moves any number of steps around the hive.

Goal: surround the enemy queen with pieces of any colour.
